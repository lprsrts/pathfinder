Pathfinder

Pathfinder makes sure you understand the work you do with AI. It answers, checks you, and keeps an honest record of what you understood.

It runs in your terminal, on your own computer, with your own ChatGPT account. It’s invite-only for now: ask Alper to add your email address.

Install (Mac)

curl -fsSL https://pathfinder.lprsrts.com/install.sh | sh

One program, harness, in ~/.local/bin, signed and notarised by Apple.

Start

  1. Connect your model account (this opens your browser): harness auth login chatgpt
  2. In the folder you’re working in, start Pathfinder: harness tui
  3. Choose whether to share usage data. It’s optional, and Pathfinder works the same either way.
  4. Press Enter to sign in with your email. Check that the page shows the same code as your terminal, then press Link this computer.

Your data

Usage events are counts and timings, never your messages. A thread is shared only when you type /share in it and confirm. harness data shows your settings, harness data off stops sending, and harness data delete erases what was sent. Privacy notice
