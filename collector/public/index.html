Pathfinder

Pathfinder makes sure you understand the work you do with AI. It points you to where your sources answer a question, checks you at the moments that matter, and keeps an honest record of what you understood, what you only claimed, and what you handed off.

It runs in your terminal, on your own computer, with your own model account. It’s free and open source: anyone can install it and use it with their own keys.

How it works

  • Sources, not answers. Ask about the folder you’re working in (code, papers, notes, PDFs) and Pathfinder shows where it addresses your question, with a short line quoted from there and checked against the file. Nothing in the folder? It searches OpenAlex, arXiv and the web the same way. You do the reading. /tell gives a real answer when you want one, recorded as told.
  • Say when something has clicked. You get a short candidate that is either right or has exactly one real mistake, and you say which and why. Passing is the only thing that counts as understanding.
  • While you judge, nothing gives it away. A gate checks every reply against the answer before you see it.
  • Hand work to the agent when you understand it, and review the result. /why shows your record.

What you need

  • A Mac (Apple silicon or Intel). On Linux, build it from source.
  • A paid ChatGPT plan (Plus or Pro), or an Anthropic or OpenAI API key. Pathfinder’s use counts against it.
  • For the gate (what keeps an answer from slipping out while you judge it): a decision key from TypeSafe, OpenAI or Google. pathfinder auth set typesafe adds one.

There’s also a small invite-only beta, with a shared gate so you don’t need a decision key, for people helping me test it.

Install

curl -fsSL https://pathfinder.lprsrts.com/install.sh | sh

One program, pathfinder, in ~/.local/bin by default (PATHFINDER_BIN_DIR or XDG_BIN_HOME choose another folder), signed and notarised by Apple.

Start

  1. Connect your model account (this opens your browser): pathfinder auth login chatgpt
  2. In the folder you’re working in, start Pathfinder: pathfinder
  3. Join the beta with your email. Sharing usage data is a separate, optional choice.

The beta guide has what to try and the known issues. /bug what happened reports a problem, after showing you everything it would send.

Your data

Your messages and files go to your model provider, under your account. Usage events, if you share them, are counts and timings, never your messages. A thread is shared only when you type /share in it and confirm. pathfinder data shows your settings, pathfinder data off stops sending, and pathfinder data delete erases what was sent.
